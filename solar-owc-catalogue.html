Odour Control: Four stages to the vent, zero smell at the boundary

Odour complaints are why installed composting machines fall silent within a few years. The DRAVYA treats odour as a primary engineering requirement: a sealed vessel under suction with four stages of exhaust treatment, verified odour-free at the property boundary.

STAGE 01

Negative Pressure Suction

Continuous suction keeps the entire vessel under vacuum; all door seams draw inward.

STAGE 02

Condensate Knock-Out

Heat exchanger condenses hot steam into liquid; water-soluble fatty acids drain out.

STAGE 03

Virgin Activated Carbon

1050+ IV virgin coal carbon bed strips hydrogen sulphides and volatile mercaptans.

STAGE 04

Impregnated Polishing Bed

Acid/permanganate chemisorption bed completely captures ammonia and amines.
