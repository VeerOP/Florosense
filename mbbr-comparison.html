Aeration: ECOVRY Micro & Nanobubbles with 88% OTE vs Standard Coarse Aeration

Standard aeration devices release 4–6 mm macro-bubbles that rush to the surface in 15 seconds, losing over 80% of pumped oxygen directly into the atmosphere. ECOVRY's proprietary Nanobubble generators inject gas cores under 200 nm. Governed by Brownian motion rather than buoyancy, these ultra-fine bubbles remain suspended indefinitely, achieving up to 88% Oxygen Transfer Efficiency (OTE) and cutting continuous blower power requirements by 35%.

The ECOVRY Physics Advantage: Nanobubble & Microbubble Gas Dissolution

Traditional MBBR blowers pump massive volumes of air through coarse diffusers. Because large bubbles rise at 0.23 m/s, bacteria have barely 15–20 seconds to extract oxygen before the bubble vents into the room. ECOVRY's sub-200nm nanobubbles experience virtually zero buoyant rise velocity. Governed by continuous Brownian motion, they form an ultra-dense, stable colloidal gas suspension that stays in the reactor for hours. This scales Oxygen Transfer Efficiency (OTE) from 15% up to 88%, guarantees continuous dissolved oxygen (DO) saturation throughout high organic peaks, and reduces continuous blower electricity bills by 35%.
